Research networks & membership

HUMAN-IT – COST Action 24167

Member, Working Group 3: Social Transformations, Policy and the New Job Market
October 2026–present

HUMAN-IT is a European research network examining how AI and disruptive technologies are reshaping work and the job market. In Working Group 3, I contribute research on labour market inclusion, skills and digital transformation, using econometrics, machine learning and causal inference.

Relevant study 1: Explainable machine learning research on informal employment in The Gambia examines how gender, livelihood strategies and location shape exclusion from formal work.

Relevant study 2: Ongoing research asks whether skills and job availability predict occupational transitions and destination occupations.

Proposed WG3 contribution: Develop a reproducible analytical framework linking workers’ skills, available jobs and occupational transitions to identify groups at risk of exclusion as AI reshapes labour demand.
