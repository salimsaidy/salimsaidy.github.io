Featured project · Agent-based modelling

Skills, Job Capacity and Occupational Mobility

In progress

Agent-based model of monthly occupational mobility in the US, exploring how skills and job availability shape where workers move.

How the model works

Simulated workers leave jobs, search, apply to new occupations and get hired. The model can represent them competing for a limited number of vacancies or receiving independent job offers.

Research question & data

The aim is to understand how much occupational skill requirements and job availability help explain worker mobility.

The model is built on linked US Current Population Survey (CPS) data and O*NET occupational profiles.

Collaboration: Ongoing research with Mahat Maalim Ibrahim.
