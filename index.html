About

I am a Research Fellow at the Center for Savings Finance and a doctoral researcher in economics at Ibn Haldun University in Istanbul. I study how digital finance and institutions shape financial access, household resilience and economic opportunity, with a focus on Sub-Saharan Africa and the developing world. My work combines econometrics, causal machine learning and text analysis to examine who benefits from financial and technological change, and where barriers remain.

My current research also examines occupational transitions, skills and job availability, and how AI and disruptive technologies reshape labour-market inclusion. At the Center for Savings Finance, I am also studying liquidity risk in Türkiye's interest-free savings finance sector. I use a simulation model calibrated to companies' published accounts to test whether the sector depends on new business and how well the regulatory limits would warn of a funding gap. I am a member of HUMAN-IT – COST Action 24167, Working Group 3: Social Transformations, Policy and the New Job Market (October 2026–present), and have published five peer-reviewed journal articles.

Journal article · 2025

Modeling drivers of fintech adoption in Sub-Saharan Africa using machine learning ↗

Drawing on the 2021 Global Findex survey across 36 Sub-Saharan African countries, this study combines econometric and machine-learning models to understand FinTech adoption. Formal account ownership, mobile phones, and internet connectivity emerge as strong predictors, with important differences across income groups, education levels, and locations.

Journal article · 2026

Islamic finance development and FinTech innovation: pathways to financial inclusion in OIC and non-OIC countries ↗

Using evidence from 64 countries, this study examines how Islamic finance development and digital innovation relate to financial inclusion. It finds that institutional development supports Islamic FinTech innovation, while wider access to financial services also depends on translating that capacity into usable, accessible products.

Journal article · 2026

Institutional quality, FDI effectiveness, and economic growth in the Gambia ↗

This study examines foreign direct investment, institutional quality, and economic growth in The Gambia using annual data from 1996 to 2023. Both investment and institutional quality are positively associated with growth, but the analysis finds no statistically significant evidence that institutional quality strengthens the effect of foreign investment.

Featured project · Simulation modelling

Growth Dependence and Liquidity Risk in Interest-Free Savings Finance

In progress

A monthly simulation model of savings finance companies, calibrated to published financial statements. It tests whether Türkiye's interest-free savings finance system needs new business to pay its allocations, and which regulatory limit (the 2026 liquidity adequacy ratio, the financing cap or the equity floors) would warn first if growth slows.

Research at the Center for Savings Finance, Ibn Haldun University.
