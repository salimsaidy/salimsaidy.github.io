Teaching & mentoring

Teaching

As a Graduate Teaching Assistant/Fellow in the Department of Economics at Ibn Haldun University from September 2021 to June 2025, I taught and supported undergraduate and graduate instruction in applied econometrics, macroeconomics and microeconomics.

I guided students through model specification, software implementation and the interpretation of results, and reviewed their analytical work. This experience connects closely with my own research practice: choosing methods that fit the question and explaining what the evidence can tell us.
