About the data

  • Source. All figures come from the results files published by the IEC: . Every constituency row was checked against the IEC's administrative-area totals and national totals, and all four elections add up exactly.
  • Constituencies. There were 48 constituencies in 2006 and 2011 and 53 in 2016 and 2021. The 2015 re-demarcation split Kombo North, Kombo Central, Serekunda East and Serekunda Central into nine constituencies.
  • Boundaries. Boundaries come from geoBoundaries and are simplified for the web. No official boundary file exists for the nine constituencies created in 2015, so they are built from ward areas using the IEC's ward list. The lines between Sanneh Mentereng, Old Yundum and Busumbala are approximate.
  • Shares and turnout. Shares are of valid votes, meaning the sum of candidate votes. Turnout is ballots cast divided by registered voters, as published. In 2011 and 2016 the two totals differ slightly in some rows.
  • Colours. Each constituency takes the colour of the party whose candidate won it. Adama Barrow stood for Coalition 2016 in 2016 and for the NPP in 2021; both are shown in the same light grey.
  • Dates. The 2006 election was held on 22 September 2006, although the IEC's file is titled "24th November 2006".
